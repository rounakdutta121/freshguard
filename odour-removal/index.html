Who the service is for

For Your Home or the Properties You Manage

Homeowners may want to use a room comfortably again. Landlords and property managers may need to plan treatment between occupants. Businesses may need to coordinate access around staff, visitors or opening hours.

Tell us the purpose of the space and any timing requirements when enquiring. Availability and treatment arrangements can then be discussed before a booking is made.

Private homes often need treatment around daily routines, pets and soft furnishings. Share whether the smell is strongest in one room or noticeable throughout the property, and whether soft materials such as carpets, curtains or upholstery are involved.

Rental and managed properties benefit from clear timing between tenancies. Let us know handover dates, access arrangements and any cleaning or repairs already completed so the proposed work fits the vacancy window.

For offices, hospitality and other commercial spaces, access may need to be planned outside busy hours. Describe opening times, who will be on site, and whether disinfection should be assessed alongside the odour enquiry.

When you contact FreshGuard, include your postcode, property type and a short description of the smell. That information helps us discuss suitability, preparation and a quotation that matches your situation.

Frequently asked questions

Questions About OdourRemoval and Disinfection

Can you help with smoke or pet smells?

Describe the type of smell, affected rooms and materials. These details help establish whether the proposed treatment is suitable and what additional preparation may be needed.

Will one treatment remove every smell permanently?

Results depend on the source, materials, severity and whether the cause has been addressed. Ask what outcome is realistic for your property and whether further work on the source is needed.

Is odour treatment the same as disinfection?

No. An odour enquiry and a disinfection requirement should be assessed separately, even if they concern the same space. Explain your goal so the proposed work matches it.

Can people or pets remain in the room?

Access restrictions depend on the treatment used. Follow the specific preparation, ventilation and re-entry instructions provided for the work. Do not assume a treated room is immediately ready for use.

What if the smell is connected to dampness or mould?

Mention this in your enquiry. Moisture investigation or mould treatment may need to be considered alongside odour work, especially if the underlying problem remains unresolved.

What determines the cost?

The affected space, odour source, materials, access and proposed treatment influence the quotation. Describe the situation to request a price for your property rather than relying on a generic room rate.
